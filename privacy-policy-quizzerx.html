QuizzerX – Your Privacy is Our Priority
📌 Last Updated: August 04, 2026

📌 1. Introduction

QuizzerX (“we”, “our”, “app”) is an educational quiz application designed to help users test and improve their knowledge across various categories. Protecting your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. By using this app, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

📊 2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile Information — If you choose to create a profile, we collect your name and profile picture (optional).
  • Quiz Answers — Your answers to quiz questions are stored to track your scores and progress.
  • Feedback — Any feedback or responses you send to us.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information — Model, OS version, unique device ID (Firebase Installation ID).
  • App Usage Data — Which categories are played most, scores, number of quizzes completed, crash reports.
  • IP Address — Approximate location and security purposes.

2.3 Firebase & App Data

  • Scores & History — Your quiz scores, number of quizzes completed, and points earned.
  • Achievements & Badges — The achievements you have unlocked.
  • Streak Information — How many consecutive days you have used the app.
  • Settings Preferences — Sound, timer, dark mode, and other preferences.

🎯 3. Why We Use Your Information

To track your quiz progress and scores
To provide customized quiz experiences
To unlock achievements and badges
To improve app performance and security
To respond to your questions or complaints
To send necessary notifications (if enabled)
To prevent fraud and misuse

🤝 4. Information Sharing (Who We Share With)

We never sell or transfer your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes. However, information may be shared in the following cases:

  • Legal Requirements: Court orders, valid requests from law enforcement agencies.
  • Service Providers: Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Analytics — which have their own privacy policies.
  • With Your Consent: If you choose to participate in any special features.

🛡️ 5. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • 🔐 Encryption: Sensitive information is stored in encrypted format.
  • 🔐 Firebase Security Rules: Strict access controls.
  • 🔐 Offline Storage: Your scores and settings are stored locally on your device (Room Database).
  • 🔐 Regular Audits & Updates: Regular reviews to minimize security risks.

⚠️ Please note that 100% security over the internet cannot be guaranteed. We continuously strive to maintain the highest level of protection.

👶 6. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian informs us that their child has provided us with information, we will delete it promptly.

📱 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights at any time:

Right to access and copy your data
Right to change your preferences in app settings
Right to delete all local data by uninstalling the app
Right to disable notifications
Right to withdraw consent

If you uninstall the app: All scores, settings, and progress data stored on your device will be deleted. Data stored in Firebase (such as analytics) may remain in an anonymous form.

💾 8. Data Retention

Your information is retained as long as the app is installed on your device. When you uninstall the app, all device data is removed. Firebase analytics data may be retained anonymously for up to 2 years.

🔔 9. Notifications

We use FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send notifications (if you grant permission):

  • 📢 Daily Challenge Reminders
  • 📢 Streak Reminders (if feature is active)
  • 📢 App Updates & Important Announcements

You can disable all notifications from the Settings menu.

📢 10. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We display AdMob ads to keep the app free. AdMob may show personalized ads based on your app activity and device information. If you do not want personalized ads, you can opt out through your device's Google Ad Settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

With a Premium Subscription, ads will not be displayed.
